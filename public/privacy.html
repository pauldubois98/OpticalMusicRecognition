MusicOCR Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

MusicOCR collects no personal data. Your photos and scores are processed on your device and are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy covers the MusicOCR Android app (io.github.pauldubois98.musicocr) and the MusicOCR website. MusicOCR is developed by pauldubois98 and is open source: you can check everything below in its source code.

Camera and images

MusicOCR asks for the camera only so that you can photograph sheet music. A photo is taken only when you take it, and the images you pick from your device are read only when you pick them. The music is recognized on your device, by models that run inside the app. Your images are never uploaded.

Your scores

In the app, your scores (their photos, the recognized music, your edits, and the title and composer you enter) are saved in the app's own storage on your device, so that you can reopen them. They are not backed up or sent anywhere by MusicOCR. Deleting a score removes it, and uninstalling the app removes them all.

When you export a score (MusicXML, MIDI, PNG or PDF), the app opens your device's share menu, and the file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Network use

  • App: recognition works offline. The app connects to the internet only when you choose to download the optional high-accuracy model, which comes from Hugging Face. Like any web server, Hugging Face receives the technical details of that download (such as your IP address). See the Hugging Face privacy policy.
  • Website: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its models from Hugging Face. These services receive the technical details of each request, as for any website. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Hugging Face privacy policy. MusicOCR sets no cookies.

Purchases

The app is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details. Google may share limited order information with the developer (such as an order number and the country of purchase) for accounting and refunds. See the Google privacy policy.

Children

MusicOCR collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any question about privacy, open an issue on GitHub.
